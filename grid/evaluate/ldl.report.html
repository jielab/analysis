The prediction is the arithmetic mean of every eligible training donor inside one frozen genetic-profile radius. Binary predictions are observed case counts divided by all donor counts. No fitted regression, CountHeads, risk-bin error head, or test-outcome donor pruning is used. The displayed intervals and donor dispersion are descriptive; they do not guarantee individual or conditional coverage.
