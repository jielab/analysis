GRID ABM: t2dm

Source: grid.abm.1; current completion receipt only. Historical blocknet and CountHeads reports are separate.

The prediction is the arithmetic mean of every eligible training donor inside one frozen genetic-profile radius. Binary predictions are observed case counts divided by all donor counts. No fitted regression, CountHeads, risk-bin error head, or test-outcome donor pruning is used. Unsupported people receive no ABM prediction; an explicitly labelled global donor mean is retained for population evaluation. More support is not evidence of lower personal error. The displayed intervals and donor dispersion are descriptive; they do not guarantee individual or conditional coverage. Coarse same-predicted-risk standardization is descriptive, and its overlap fractions must be read with the difference. Previously inspected tests remain exploratory.

Group evidence: verified_from_kinship_components; GWAS overlap: unverified.
